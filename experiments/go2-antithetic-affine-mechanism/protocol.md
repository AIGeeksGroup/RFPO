# H75: Cross-Policy Affine-Antisymmetry Mechanism

## Question

Does the equal-total-NFE antithetic recovery observed in H70/H73 coincide with
a stable approximately odd source-to-action displacement across all four
independently trained Go2 policies, rather than only the original seed-42
checkpoint?

## Fixed checkpoints and states

- Task: `Isaac-Velocity-Flat-Unitree-Go2-v0`.
- Fixed final `model_1499.pt` checkpoints from official training seeds
  42, 43, 44, and 45. H69 manifests determine the seed-43/44/45 paths; the H67
  checkpoint determines the seed-42 path.
- Euler integration with 32 steps, matching H70/H73.
- For each checkpoint, use 128 environments, evaluation seed `20261650`, primary
  source seed `20261651`, secondary IID-source seed `20261652`, and eight
  consecutive states generated by stepping that checkpoint's positive-source
  policy.
- At every state evaluate `F(o,0)`, `F(o,z)`, `F(o,-z)`, and `F(o,z2)`, where
  `z2` is an independently seeded Gaussian source.

No checkpoint, source scale, solver, step count, or state population may be
changed after seeing results.

## Measurements

For flattened action displacements `d+ = F(o,z)-F(o,0)` and
`d- = F(o,-z)-F(o,0)`, report separately for every training seed:

1. cosine similarity between `d+` and `d-`;
2. antithetic residual ratio
   `R_anti = RMS((F(o,z)+F(o,-z))/2-F(o,0)) / RMS(d+)`;
3. IID residual ratio
   `R_iid = RMS((F(o,z)+F(o,z2))/2-F(o,0)) / RMS(d+)`;
4. even and odd displacement RMS, with
   `even=(d+ + d-)/2` and `odd=(d+ - d-)/2`;
5. exact-negation, exact-float32-mean, finite-value, and frozen-actor checks.

Aggregate quantities are descriptive because four policies are too few for a
reliable correlation analysis with return. H70/H73 reward gains remain the
separate behavioral evidence.

## Decisions

H75 supports the proposed affine-antisymmetry mechanism only if all four fixed
policies satisfy every validity check and all of the following locked gates:

- displacement cosine is at most `-0.90`;
- `R_anti` is at most `0.20`;
- `R_anti` is at most `0.30 * R_iid`.

A failed seed refutes cross-policy mechanism stability. Do not retry another
state seed, enlarge the state population, tune the source scale, or exclude the
failed policy. H75 does not claim that affine antisymmetry is novel; Jia et al.
(arXiv:2506.06185) is the direct generative-model precedent.
